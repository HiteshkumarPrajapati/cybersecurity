# Findings Register — Internal Audit (Meridian Consulting Group)

**Audit scope:** Information security controls, sampled against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, with reference to the ACSC Essential Eight and Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) obligations where relevant.
**Audit period:** July 2026
**Auditor:** GRC Analyst (portfolio exercise)
**Audit type:** Internal audit, control walkthrough and evidence sampling

This register is the one-page version. Each row links through to the full finding write-up, which includes the evidence reviewed, root cause, and the agreed management response.

| [F-03](finding-03-sensitive-client-data-not-classified.md) | Sensitive client data not classified or labelled | Medium | ISO 27001 A.5.12, A.5.13 · Privacy Act (APP 11) | Accepted by management, remediation planned |

A quick note on the ratings above: none of these three findings were rated Critical, and that's a deliberate distinction worth explaining rather than glossing over. Critical, in how this audit rates things, is reserved for a gap that's both easy to exploit and catastrophic if it is — think an internet-facing system with a known, unpatched, actively exploited vulnerability. What's here is different in kind: two High findings that represent a genuine and material weakness with a plausible path to serious harm, and one Medium finding that's more of a foundational gap than an active exposure on its own. All three are worth fixing promptly. Not all three are the same size of fire.

## Overall audit conclusion

Meridian's control environment is broadly consistent with what you'd expect from a firm of its size and maturity — reasonable in places, thin in others, and with the classic pattern of technical capability existing but not being fully switched on or backed by a written process. None of the three findings in this audit are surprising or unusual for a professional services firm of this scale; what matters is whether they get closed on the timelines below or slide, the way these things tend to when there's no single owner holding the line. Recommend this register be reviewed again at the 90-day mark against the target dates set out in each finding.
